Session 39 — deviations and implementation choices, declared BEFORE any H11 or H10 result was computed.
Written 2026-10-19 ~17:35 UTC. At this point: Polymarket US price histories and Kalshi candles were still
downloading; no crossing, basket, P&L, settlement comparison or carry return had been computed. The only
data looked at were (a) the Polymarket US climate market listing (titles, slugs, date coverage, fee
coefficient, status), (b) ONE price-history response (tc-temp-nychigh-2026-05-01-lt61f) to learn the
format, (c) one Kalshi event's strikes (KXHIGHNY-26MAY01), and (d) the KXBTCPERP market record, the first
and last two funding rows, and four hourly candles from 2026-06-15 to learn the schema.

The pre-registration (DECISIONS.md Session 38) is unchanged. Where it is silent or a data fact forces a
choice, the choice is fixed here. Each is labelled DEVIATION (changes what the text says) or
IMPLEMENTATION (fills a gap the text leaves open).

H11
1. DEVIATION — Polymarket US locked/crossed points excluded. The opening history points of a market are a
   0.5/0.5 seed (longPrice 0.5, shortPrice 0.5, i.e. bid = ask = 0.50) for hours before any quoting. A
   resting book cannot be locked or crossed on a price-time-priority exchange, so such a point is a
   placeholder, exactly like the 1c bid the pre-registration already excludes. The pre-registration
   requires 0 < bid < ask < 1 on Kalshi but only ask < 0.99 and bid > 0.01 on Polymarket; the binding run
   also requires Polymarket bid < ask. Without it, a 0.5/0.5 seed against a Kalshi ask of 0.30 would
   "cross" by ~20c. The literal (as-written) variant is run and reported alongside, so the effect of this
   deviation is visible.
2. IMPLEMENTATION — titles. 40 Polymarket titles use a degree sign ("56° to 57°", "55° or below"). The
   title parser strips "°"; slugs are still never read for intervals.
3. IMPLEMENTATION — "all dates in the window". Binding: every calendar event date 2026-04-22 → 2026-10-17
   (179 dates), zero when nothing is jointly listed (Polymarket US lists none of the six cities on 12
   dates; PHL only from 2026-10-15). Sensitivity: only dates with at least one jointly listed city.
4. IMPLEMENTATION — episode. Consecutive hourly candles (end times 3600 s apart) on one rung and one
   basket (A or B) that both cross. An hour that is unobservable (no Polymarket point within 60 s, or
   either book not two-sided) is not a crossing hour and ends the episode. Locked profit uses the net
   cost of the episode's FIRST hour (the price on offer when first seen); the best hour is reported as an
   upper-bound sensitivity.
5. IMPLEMENTATION — pairing. "Last Polymarket point no more than 60 s before the candle end" = the
   latest point with candle_end − 60 ≤ timestamp ≤ candle_end.
6. IMPLEMENTATION — dates and periods are by EVENT date (the climate day the rung settles on), not the
   calendar date of the quote.
7. IMPLEMENTATION — settlement comparison uses Kalshi `result` (yes/no) against Polymarket US
   `outcomePrices` (["1","0"] = YES). A Kalshi `scalar` result on a matched rung is reported separately
   and counted as "not the same" for the kill criterion.
8. IMPLEMENTATION — Polymarket ladder sum-of-YES-asks (descriptive): evaluated at the same hourly marks,
   only when every rung of that Polymarket city-day has a usable ask point within 60 s and the rungs
   parse to an exhaustive, disjoint partition of the integers.

H10
9. IMPLEMENTATION — sign. Confirmed from Kalshi's own help center ("How Funding Works", dated
   2026-06-03, saved under backtest/cache/venues/primary_sources/): "If the funding rate is positive
   (perp trading above spot): long holders pay short holders." This is Kalshi's published product
   documentation, not the rulebook PDF (kalshi.com returns 429 to scripts). Treated as a primary source.
10. IMPLEMENTATION — funding received in a held week = Σ rate × mark × contracts over funding times in
   (entry 21:00, exit 21:00], i.e. 21 fundings, the exit Monday's 20:00 included and the entry Monday's
   20:00 (the decision rate) excluded.
11. IMPLEMENTATION — margin buffer = the posted perp margin, 2/L × notional (L = the frozen leverage
   estimate: BTC 6.38, ETH 4.87). Adverse move = the CUMULATIVE maximum adverse excursion from the held
   week's starting perp price (hourly ask high, since a short buys back at the ask), as a fraction of
   notional; a week fails if it exceeds 80% of the buffer. The largest single-hour move is also reported.
   Cumulative is the stricter reading and is binding.
12. IMPLEMENTATION — benchmark. DTB3 (FRED, discount basis) on the decision date (last available value),
   accrued as DTB3 × 7/360 per week. Excess = week return on capital − that accrual; flat weeks = 0.
13. IMPLEMENTATION — capital per held week = multiplier × spot notional at the week's start; quantity is
   fixed while the position continues; no rebalancing cost.
14. IMPLEMENTATION — the in-sample half needs the 2026-10-19 21:00 UTC candle to close its last week. It
   is run after that candle exists.
15. NOTE, not used — Kalshi's "How Margin Works" page says margin earns ~3.25% APY at the clearinghouse.
   The pre-registration freezes "no interest on Kalshi margin"; that stands. Reported as a sensitivity.
16. Sensitivity only, unverified: a 60 bps per side retail spot fee in place of the frozen 10 bps.
17. IMPLEMENTATION (added ~18:00 UTC, still before any H10 number) — a held week pays the exit cost (perp
   bought back at the 21:00 closing ask, spot sold, fees) only if the NEXT Monday's decision is flat;
   otherwise it is marked at mid with no cost, as pre-registered. For the last in-sample week
   (2026-10-12 -> 10-19) the next decision is the 2026-10-19 20:00 one, read from the data once it
   exists. Perp quantity = $1 / entry perp price, spot quantity = $1 / entry spot price (equal notional
   at entry), both held fixed while the position continues. Spot = Coinbase Exchange hourly candle that
   STARTS at 20:00 UTC (its close is the 21:00 price). Weekly thirds of the combined 24-week window:
   weeks 1-8, 9-16, 17-24; the in-sample half covers weeks 1-18.
