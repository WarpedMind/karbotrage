Eligibility and application process

The Market Maker Program is live, and market maker status is granted after a review of financial resources, trading experience, and business reputation. Approval is based on the ability to meet ongoing liquidity obligations while maintaining a fair and orderly market. [1]

Quoting obligations and program benefits

Market makers agree to provide consistent two‑sided liquidity. In return, they may receive:

Reduced fees
Adjusted position limits
These benefits are conditional on maintaining sufficient liquidity and orderly markets. Market makers remain subject to the same core rules and oversight as other participants and still bear trading risk.
